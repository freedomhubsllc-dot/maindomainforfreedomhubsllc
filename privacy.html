Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Freedom Hubs LLC ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website www.freedomhubsllc.com or use any of our mobile applications, AI platforms, and specialized DBA ventures (including Aura Daily Companion).

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when registering an account, configuring care network settings, or interacting with our platforms. The personal information we collect may include:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, phone number, email address, and designated care network contact details.
  • Payment Data: Billing details processed securely through our PCI-compliant third-party merchant service providers. We do not store full credit card numbers on our local servers.
  • Application & Interaction Data: Daily check-in statuses, scheduled reminders, voice/text interaction preferences, and account settings necessary to deliver automated companion services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information collected via our website and mobile applications for legitimate operational purposes, including to:

  • Operate, maintain, and deliver our AI mobile applications and automated daily check-in workflows.
  • Send authorized alerts and status updates to designated family members or care contacts based on user-configured permissions.
  • Process subscription payments and prevent unauthorized or fraudulent activity.
  • Communicate directly with you regarding customer support, technical updates, or account inquiries.
  • Comply with applicable legal, security, and regulatory obligations.

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or user interaction data to third parties for marketing purposes. We share information only under the following controlled circumstances:

  • User-Authorized Care Contacts: Status updates or check-in notifications sent strictly to contacts explicitly designated by the account holder.
  • Trusted Infrastructure & Service Providers: Cloud hosting, voice/AI processing infrastructure, and payment processors bound by strict confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by law, court order, or governmental regulation.

4. Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect user data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All web and application communications are encrypted in transit via SSL/TLS technology, with strict access controls applied to account and check-in records.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain personal account and check-in data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Users may request data export or permanent account deletion at any time.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, as well as to modify or revoke caregiver sharing permissions at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.
